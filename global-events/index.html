How I work

Led by curiosity, delivered with discipline.

Every event begins with a question: how do we want people to feel? In a crowd of thousands that feeling is energy, anticipation and release. Curiosity drives the creative, and two decades of operational rigour make sure it survives contact with reality. I own the full lifecycle: concept, production, budget and P&L, partner management, and measurement.

🧭

Curiosity first

I dig into the audience, the format and the market before a single fixture is booked. The best experiences come from asking better questions about what genuinely moves people.

⚙️

Operational backbone

Playbooks, QA systems and supplier frameworks built across 800+ events — the infrastructure that lets creative ambition scale without breaking under pressure.

⏱️

Fast mobilisation

Dropped into a live project, I map the critical path in the first week: scope, budget, risks, owners and the run-of-show. Fixed timelines and hard deadlines are the norm, not the exception.

Public events & competitions

Festivals, concerts, competitions — and the machine behind them.

Most of my work is public-facing and large-scale: music festivals, street festivals and concerts, sporting and esports competitions, and retail activations for audiences of tens of thousands. The formats change; the disciplines don't. Every one demands technical and AV production, site logistics, marketing and ticketing, commercial partners — and a large multidisciplinary team that has to move as one.

Music festivals & concerts Street festivals 3x3 basketball tournaments Esports tournaments Mall & retail activations Film festival International summits
🎚️

Technical & AV production

Staging and rigging, sound and lighting design, LED and screens, power and distribution, broadcast and live streaming, plus the technical rehearsals and redundancy that keep a show running when something fails.

🚧

Logistics & site operations

Site build and load-in/load-out schedules, crowd flow and capacity planning, barriers, security and stewarding, permits and licensing, health and safety, and contractor and vendor management on the ground.

📣

Marketing, promotion & ticketing

Campaign and promotional partnerships, ticketing platforms and box office, pricing and access tiers, accreditation and guest lists, and accountability for attendance, footfall and engagement targets.

👥

Leading large teams

Building and running multidisciplinary crews — production, technical, front-of-house, stewards, volunteers and department heads — with clear roles, briefings, comms channels and a run-of-show everyone works to. I led a 140-person agency at peak.

🏆

Competition operations

Format and bracket design, seeding and scheduling, player and team registration, referees, officials and rulesets, results, disputes and prize administration — applied across 3x3 basketball and esports alike.

🛍️

Brand & retail partnerships

Sponsor integration and brand activations inside malls and public venues, built to hit footfall, dwell-time and engagement targets — working within mall operating hours, load-in windows, sound limits and permits.

In practice that has meant the KitKat 3x3 basketball tournament in Sharjah (2013), esports tournaments and in-mall gaming activations across Dubai (2014–2018), and public festival franchises including Street Nights, the Marina Music Festival and the Dubai Chocolate Festival — each with its own audience, technical footprint and commercial partners.
