How I work

Led by curiosity, accountable for the outcome.

Every event begins with a question: how do we want people to feel? In a crowd of thousands that feeling is energy, anticipation and release. Curiosity drives the creative — and a commercial head makes sure it earns its place, on budget and against the objectives the client is actually measured on.

🧭

Curiosity first

I dig into the audience, the market and the commercial objective before a single fixture is booked. The best experiences come from asking better questions about what genuinely moves people.

🎯

Commercially accountable

Creative ambition is judged against the numbers it has to hit — attendance, footfall, sponsorship value, cost per attendee, margin. I own that conversation rather than hand it to someone else.

⏱️

Fast mobilisation

Dropped into a live project, I map the critical path in the first week: scope, budget, risks, owners and run-of-show. Fixed timelines and hard deadlines are the norm, not the exception.
