On Saturday, October 25th, during Amsterdam Dance Event, the Auditorium and Oosterbar will be transformed into two completely different musical dimensions.*

📚 *Upstairs – The Lecture Hall*
Happylives, MelodicRoom, Sectore 6, and Technoport will guide you through an uplifting, progressive and melodic soundscape – a masterclass in dance music.

⚡ *Downstairs – Detention in the Basement*
In the dark, intimate underground of Oosterbar, Off Global Group from Bahrain and Stuck in a Loop from Amsterdam take control. Expect relentless, high-energy techno that keeps you “after school” until the early hours.
