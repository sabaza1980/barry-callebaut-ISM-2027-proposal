The Concept

One Room. Two Souls.

Melodic Room is an electronic music label and event concept specialized in Melodic House & Techno — bringing together dark and euphoric elements through global and local talent. What began as a theme-based club night in Cairo grew into a label and a touring showcase with one obsession: quality of sound over hype.

Black

The deep end of the room. Dark, emotional Melodic Techno & House — hypnotic, driving, introspective. Visuals, light and sound tuned to the shadows.

Deep · Dark · Emotional

White

The bright side of the same heartbeat. Happy, uplifting progressive sounds for the cheerful part of the crowd — melody first, hands in the air.

Uplifting · Progressive · Euphoric
