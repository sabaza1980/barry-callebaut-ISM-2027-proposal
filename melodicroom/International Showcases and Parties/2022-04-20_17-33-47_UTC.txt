Hello Montreal !!!

We are happy to present "Dome Of Faith"!!

The excitement is through the roof to partner up with Melodic Room for the the launch of a new musical concept in the melodic techno industry, in North America. 

This time, we are happy to party with few international artists who have made a long run in this industry around the world. 

We will be Opening our night with @hickykalo. Rising out of Montreal's prospering nightlife. This duo has been dictating the sounds of the city's underground scene over the last half decade.

Then, we will be taking you on a trip with @soel__music. He has been an electronic and ambient producer and DJ, having played in 17+ countries worldwide, and producing more than 38 hours of music. He has released twice on Afterlife Records, and a sold-out vinyl EP on Recondite's label, Plangent Records and Siamese (Adriatique's label).

Then, we will be closing our night with @citizenkain. It didn't take millenaries, nor one century but only half a decade for the french DJ to divert us from it and to demonstrate the evenly universality of his compositions. Thus it's not surprising to see the variety, as his talents are that polyvalent, of the personalities gaging his production: Solomun, Tale Of Us, Maceo Plex, Carl Cox and many more.
