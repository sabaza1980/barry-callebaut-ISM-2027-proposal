Mısır Merkezli plak şirketi Melodic Room, Cairo‘dan 2020 Eskişehir’e uçuyor!
Atina merkezli prodüktör ve DJ ikilisi Dizharmonia, elektronik müziğe getirdiği muhteşem yorumuyla sizi eşsiz ve unutulmaz bir yolculuğa çıkaracak. Melodic Room Showcase: Dizharmonia, KA:US, Barış Berber

Egypt-based record label Melodic Room is flying from Cairo to Eskişehir 2020!
Athens-based producer and DJ duo Dizharmonia will take you on a unique and unforgettable journey with their magnificent interpretation of electronic music.  Dizharmonia, who previously worked with world-famous record labels such as Stil Vor Talent, Ritter Butzke and Melodic Room, will be on the Eskişehir 2020 stage on May 7.

KA:US, one of the successful Turkish producers of the Melodic Room record label, and Barış Berber, one of the powerful names of the local stages, who continue to perform in Istanbul's important nightclubs, will accompany this special night.
